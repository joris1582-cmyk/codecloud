## Posting strategy (from market research)
- **Hook in the first 2–3 seconds.** Each part opens with a big "PART X" plus its title. Keep that as the cover image.
- **Watch time and rewatches matter most.** Each part ends on a cliffhanger with a "PART X+1 — follow" end card.
- **Post the parts spaced out.** Space them 3–6 hours apart, or one a day. Pin Part 1 on your profile and add every part to a TikTok "Series"/playlist called *Kira the Triceratops*.
- **Keywords in caption, on screen and in the voice-over.** TikTok search reads all three, so use "dinosaur story", "triceratops" and "t-rex".
- **AI label:** the narration is an AI voice. TikTok requires the "AI-generated content" toggle for this. The toggle is a label in the post settings, not text in the caption, and per TikTok it does not reduce reach.
- **Mix of hashtags:** 2–3 broad (#fyp #foryou), 3–4 niche (#dinotok #dinosaur #triceratops #trex), 2 format (#storytime #animation).
