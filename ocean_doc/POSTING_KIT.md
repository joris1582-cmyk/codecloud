## Facts used
- Sunlight does not reach below about 1,000 m (start of the midnight zone).
- Challenger Deep, Mariana Trench: about 10,935 m, about 1,070 times surface pressure (shown on screen).
- Deep-sea anglerfish lure prey with a glowing lure in front of the mouth.
- Giant squid (Architeuthis dux) were first filmed alive in their habitat in 2012.
- Only a tiny fraction of the deep seafloor has been seen directly; a 2025 estimate puts it near 0.001 %.
